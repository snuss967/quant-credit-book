## 0. What the three lenses agree on

Three readers who do not share a vocabulary reached one verdict: Edition 1 is a superb reference written by, and for, the person who *designs* the engines, and it skips three stretches of the reader's actual life. The RSSG partner finds the eighteen months **between the LMT and the effective date** missing (plan value, DIP economics, §1129, the room's economics, cross-border forums, tax). The desk head finds the **hours between 6:45 and 9:30 a.m.** missing (risk pack, explain, limits, TCA, promotion ladder, governance). The transition coach finds the **months before day one and the year after** missing (interviews, dictionary, ramp, gates, solutions, portfolio). All three gaps are, by coincidence of the user's directives, exactly the kind of content the user asked for more of: longer stories, mundane quant-shop life, other investors' perspectives, intuition before formula, and "knowing when to apply it." The new files below are therefore written as *scenes with engines in them*, not as manuals, and every one obeys the Voice Amendments (Math, Gently at first use; View From Another Seat; When to Apply It; code in Appendix F; three figures; specific episodes in the Resource Graph).

**Naming rules this synthesis fixes.** Appendix F is `95_appF_code_companion.md` (user-created) and stays. Reviewer proposals that claimed "App F" are renumbered. Appendix Z (`99_appZ_…`) is reserved for solutions so it sorts last whatever letters G–K become.

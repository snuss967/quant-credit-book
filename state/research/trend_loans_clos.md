| Mar 2023 (sample 2009–2018; CreditFlux CLO-i, 52–68% coverage) | Kundu, "The externalities of fire sales: evidence from CLOs." OC/ID = (par + defaulted collateral value + purchase price of discount obligations − CCC excess adjustment) / (par of tranche + all senior). CCC/Caa1 loans above the typical 7.5% limit are marked at the *lowest* market value in the CCC bucket; discount obligations are carried at purchase price until they trade above about 90c for more than 30 days. CLOs ran within 3% of the interest-diversion (ID) threshold over 2009–2018, and the median CLO sits within 4% of its most stringent covenant. | ESRB Working Paper No. 141 (Mar 2023), pp. 2, 7–8 |
| Mar 2023 (O&G shock 2014) | Each +1pp of a firm's CLO-routed O&G exposure: secondary loan price −61 to −180bp; primary loan spread +18 to +22bp (4–5% of average spread); bond spread +28 to +36bp; selling $1m moves price 180–404bp (Amihud). The average CCC/Caa1 loan is priced at 85c, but the cheapest CCC loan in each CLO averages 58c. The haircut therefore rewards selling higher-priced CCCs ("contractual arbitrage"). 82% of defaulted loans were rated CCC/Caa1 or below. | Kundu, ESRB WP 141 (Mar 2023), pp. 2–3, 8, 30–33, 37–38 |

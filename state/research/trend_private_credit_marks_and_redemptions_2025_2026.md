## 1. Headline trends

1. **The run became a queue.** In the first three quarters of 2026, tender requests at eight evergreen vehicles ran from about 9% to 40% of shares per quarter against 5% caps, and the Fed counts Q1 2026 as the first quarter in which accepted redemptions exceeded inflows at perpetual BDCs.
2. **The gate held, so the fill rate became the price.** BCRED filled about half of $4.5B requested in Q2 and carried a $2.3B backlog into Q3; OCIC's fill rate went 22.8% to 26.6% to about 30% as requests fell from 21.9% to 16.8% of shares; Barings and OTIC stayed at 44-47% and 13-14%.
3. **Demand tracked exposure, not the asset class.** The software-focused OTIC saw 38-40% of shares tendered in two straight quarters; HPS's HLEND saw 9-13%. Peak demand was Q1 at OCIC but Q2 at BCRED, ADS, HLEND and ASIF.
4. **Marks barely moved while prices did.** BCRED's mark went 96.1 to 95.4 and large BDC NAVs fell 2-4% year to date, yet the median externally managed listed BDC trades at about 0.76 of book, FSK at 0.56, and a bid for OBDC II units came in 32% below NAV, while institutions bought $1.4B of Blue Owl loans at 99.8% of par.
5. **Credit-quality headlines depend on the basis.** Non-accruals at cost are about twice those at fair value (3.5x at OBDC); Moody's default rate for middle-market CLO obligors is 4.0% reported but 5.8% with PIK and extension events; PIMCO's BDC shadow default rate is about 19%; Proskauer's index says 2.51%.
6. **Sponsors have a five-item remedy menu:** upsizing the cap with house money (BCRED, 7%), return of capital in place of tenders (OBDC II, 35% of NAV), par loan sales to institutions, listed-share buybacks (OBDC at 86% of book) and distribution cuts (BCRED 10.0% to 9.1%).
7. **Marks are being turned into prices by infrastructure:** Apollo's daily pricing and asset-level pricing from 30 Oct 2026, 5,000+ ICE IDs, PRIV's firm-bid wrapper (which SEC staff questioned in Feb 2025), Z.1 private-credit series and the SEC's move to quarterly N-PORT.
8. **Public credit is diverging at the bottom.** HY OAS is 3.2% but CCC OAS is 12.8% (up 308bp since June), the Fed hiked 25bp on 17 Sep 2026, and Moody's puts Caa-equivalents at more than half of 2027-28 middle-market maturities.

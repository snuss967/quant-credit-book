**F1. Barzykin & Ciceri (HSBC), arXiv:2604.20406, "Bond Market Making with a Hit-Ratio Target"** [fetched arxiv.org/abs/2604.20406 and the full PDF, 2026-10-18]. v1 22 Apr 2026, v2 27 Jul 2026; q-fin.RM; MSC 93E20, 91B24, 91B70; 16 pages, 9 figures; no journal ref listed. Authors affiliated with HSBC, 8 Canada Square (ack. R. Anthony, J. Freeland, A. Cottrell; views not HSBC's).
- Setup: bonds m=1..M, mid dS=sigma dB; client tiers tau in T, targeted subset A; size ladder z_k; RFQ arrival intensity lambda^{s,k}_{m,tau}; fill probability f(delta); fill intensity Lambda = lambda f(delta) (eqs 1-3). Size-weighted instantaneous hit ratio r_tau(delta) = (1/W_tau) sum z_k Lambda (eq 7), W_tau = sum z_k lambda (eq 6). Objective (8): sup E[ X_T + Q'S_T - (eta/2) Q'Sigma Q - int ( (phi/2) Q'Sigma Q - sum_{tau in A} (kappa_tau W_tau / 2)(r_tau - r*_tau)^2 ) dt ]. NB the sign convention: penalty on squared deviation of an instantaneous hit ratio, a Markovian surrogate for the reporting-window KPI (the paper says a literal ratio-of-counts target needs cumulative-count state variables and breaks tractability).
- Key result 1 (dualization): -(kappa/2)(r-r*)^2 = inf_xi [ xi (r - r*) + xi^2/(2 kappa) ] (eq 12), so the HJB stays separable across (bond, tier, side, size): Hamiltonian H(p) = sup_delta Lambda(delta)(delta - p) (eq 14); exact optimal control delta* = f^{-1}( -H'(p)/lambda ) with p = Delta u - xi_tilde for targeted tiers, p = Delta u for untargeted (eqs 15-18). Dual closure (19): xi_tilde(t,q) = kappa_tau ( r*_tau + (1/W_tau) sum z_k H'(p) ) (implicit, since p depends on xi).
- Key result 2 (quadratic/BEGV approximation, after Bergault-Evangelista-Gueant-Vieira): u(t,q) ~ -(1/2) q'A(t)q - C(t); matrix Riccati A' = A D A - phi Sigma, A(T) = eta Sigma (eq 29) with D = diag(d_m), d_m = sum_{tau,s,k} z_k H''(0) (eq 30), so ALL tiers (targeted or not) contribute to inventory curvature; closed form A(t) = D^{-1/2} G [ (sinh(tG) + (eta/phi... ) G cosh) ... ] D^{-1/2} (eq 32), stationary A = sqrt(phi) D^{-1/2} (D^{1/2} Sigma D^{1/2})^{1/2} D^{-1/2} (eq 33). Dual closure becomes inventory-independent in the symmetric case: xi_tilde = kappa_tilde y_tau with 1/kappa_tilde = 1/kappa + (1/W) sum z_k H''(0) (eq 27) and y_tau = r* + (1/W) sum z_k H'(0) + (1/2W) sum z_k^2 A_mm H''(0) (eq 28). Asymmetric intensities add a linear term B(t)'q and a q-dependent dual (Appendix A, eqs 41-46).
- Key result 3 (linearized quotes): riskless spread delta_0 = argmax Lambda(delta) delta (eq 34); delta* ~ delta_0 + (1/c)[ e_m'A(+/-q + z_k e_m /2) - xi_tilde ] for targeted, same without xi for untargeted (eq 38), where c = 2 - f f''/(f')^2 evaluated at delta_0 (eq 36); H'(0) = -Lambda(delta_0), H''(0) = Lambda(delta_0)/(delta_0 c) (eq 37). So the paper's own decomposition has THREE terms: riskless spread + inventory-risk correction + hit-ratio correction (NOT five; the five-term decomposition is Niang 2026, F2).
- Numerics: single bond, single tier; size ladder z=(1,5,20) mm; intensities lambda=(500,200,50)/day; sigmoid fill with alpha=(2,1.5,1), beta=(2,1.5,1) per bp; r*=0.1; phi=1; T=1 day; qmax=100mm; eta=0; explicit Euler in JAX; kappa=10 or 100. Findings: BEGV with exact nonlinear quote map and exact xi(q) closure is "impressively accurate"; constant closure xi_0 good only for small inventories because exact xi(q) is approximately quadratic in q (Fig 3); a non-targeted "background tier" shares inventory risk, so the targeted tier's skew falls, the riskless spread at q=0 widens and the hit ratio for given kappa improves (Figs 6-7; background intensity 10x targeted); an offsetting correlated background bond (rho=0.8) cuts the skew and raises the hit ratio (Figs 8-9). Spending on the target "generally implies less P&L". All numerics are synthetic: no real RFQ data.

**F2. Niang, arXiv:2605.30643, "Quality-Adjusted Hit-Ratio Targeting in Corporate Bond Market Making" (subtitle on PDF: "Credit Alpha, Client Flow Quality, and Style-Aware Warehousing")** [abs page + full PDF read 2026-10-18]. SINGLE AUTHOR: Bouna Niang (the book's earlier "confirm authors" flag resolves to one author). v1 28 May 2026 (PDF dated 1 Jun 2026), q-fin.TR primary with q-fin.RM, CC BY 4.0, 17 pages, no journal ref. Footnote: published "in a personal capacity as an independent quantitative trader/researcher"; disclaims views of "any former employer, including HSBC and Citi". Builds directly on Barzykin-Ciceri (F1) and cites Avellaneda-Stoikov and GLFT. NO proprietary data; all numerics synthetic.
- Markout decomposition (eq 10): M_raw = M_factor + M_carry/rolldown + M_issuerRV + M_index/ETF + eps; only the residual eps defines toxicity chi_i^res = E[eps | bucket i, fill] (eq 11). Quality weight w_i = exp(-gamma chi_i^res) (eq 13). Quality-adjusted instantaneous hit ratio r^Q_tau = sum z_i w_i Lambda_i / W^Q_tau, W^Q = sum z_i w_i lambda_i (eq 14). Same dualization as BC: -(kW/2)(r-r*)^2 = inf_xi W[xi(r-r*) + xi^2/(2k)] (eq 16).
- Prop 1: after dualization the bucket contribution is z_i H_i( p_i[u] + chi_i^res - w_i xi_tau ) (eq 18); toxicity and the target enter as a scalar SHIFT of the Hamiltonian argument. Exact dual: xi_tau = kappa_tau [ r^Q,* + (1/W^Q) sum z_i w_i H_i'(p0_i - w_i xi) ] (eq 21) = kappa (r* - r^Q(xi)) (eq 22), p0_i = p_i[u] + chi_i^res. Prop 2: F(xi) = xi - kappa[...] has F' = 1 + (kappa/W^Q) sum z_i w_i^2 H_i'' > 0, so a unique root exists, solved by 1-D root finder (Newton in the simulations).
- Closed form for logistic fill f(delta) = 1/(1+exp(alpha + beta delta)): x(p) = W0(exp(-alpha - beta p - 1)) (Lambert W, principal branch), delta*(p) = p + (1+x)/beta (eqs 25-27), H'(p) = -lambda x/(1+x), H''(p) = lambda beta x/(1+x)^3 (eq 28).
- FIVE-TERM quote decomposition (eq 37): quote offset = riskless spread + inventory skew - credit-alpha skew + residual-toxicity charge - quality-hit-ratio subsidy. Riccati A' = A D A - phi Sigma (eq 33), D_mm = sum z_i H_i''(pbar_i^ref) (eq 34), stationary A = sqrt(phi) D^{-1/2}(D^{1/2} Sigma D^{1/2})^{1/2} D^{-1/2} (eq 35); linear alpha term ell = (AD)^{-1} mu (eq 36). Full quote argument pbar_i = p_i + chi_i^res - w_i xi_tau (eq 32). NOTE for the book: the 5 terms are Niang's, the 3 terms are BC's.
- Synthetic design: d=20 EUR IG bonds with factor covariance Sigma = B Omega B' + Sigma_eps; two flow types (carry/RV low residual toxicity; residual-toxic); Monte Carlo 300 paths per target/policy for the frontier, 100 paths per policy for the 8% benchmark over five trading days; PnL in bp*MM per day.
- Table 1 (frontier, raw target -> attained quality HR, PnL/day): 4% -> 4.14%, +7.32; 6% -> 5.64%, -2.66; 8% -> 7.21%, -15.88; 10% -> 8.87%, -33.33; 12% -> 10.65%, -55.36. Residual QA + alpha: 4% -> 3.56%, +14.98; 6% -> 4.89%, +12.11; 8% -> 6.38%, +5.74; 10% -> 7.96%, -2.58; 12% -> 9.64%, -14.22.
- Table 2 (matched attained quality): savings of residual-QA over raw rise from 6.41 (at 4.14% HR) to 28.63 bp*MM/day (at 9.64% HR).
- Table 3 (8% target attribution, QA minus raw, bp*MM/day): spread capture 34.03 -> 24.54 (-9.49); residual toxicity cost 41.73 -> 13.46 (+28.26 saved); terminal liquidation 6.02 -> 4.35 (+1.67); net 19.57 saving (raw -14.32, QA +5.25).
- Table 4 (8% target flow allocation): raw target wins carry/RV 7.29% and residual-toxic 7.47% (i.e. it does NOT discriminate); residual QA wins 7.05% and 0.87%. Residual cost/day 41.73 vs 13.46. "Naive gross toxicity" (using raw markouts) cuts residual-toxic HR to 0.61% but ALSO carry/RV to 1.08% with PnL +8.22 and lowest risk, i.e. over-widens (a caution: gross-markout screens throw out good flow).
- Table 5/6: raw PnL std 13.21, 5%-ile -35.14; QA std 10.78, 5%-ile -12.50; terminal inventory risk 25.73 vs 20.08.
- Table 7: saving 19.77 (liquidation cost 0 bp) to 30.38 bp*MM/day (2.00 bp), CI widths about +/-1.3: not an artefact of one liquidation assumption.
- Section 10 extension (style-aware warehousing, Sweep/PT fills random with fraction phi in [0,1]): risk-aware participation pi* = clip[ phibar (Y(ell - Aq) + |Y|(b - c)) / (phi2 Y^2 A_mm) ] in [0, pi_max] (eq 44); at 8% target PnL 8.47 vs 5.85 (no sweep) vs 6.28 (naive sweep) vs 7.22 (fixed style sweep); right-way sweep share 81.9% vs 59.4% naive; terminal inventory risk 19.3 vs 20.9. 
- Self-declared limitations: synthetic; residual toxicity only as good as the markout adjustment (omit carry/rolldown/RV and you misclassify sophisticated clients as toxic); extension is reduced-form. Calibration roadmap lists arrival intensities, fill curves, factor covariance, alpha, residual toxicity from adjusted markouts, and "archetypal tiers" (carry/RV tier 1-2, passive/index tier 1, residual-toxic tier 3).
- Replication status: no code or data repository mentioned in the text I read; the paper offers a "reproducible research design for later calibration". Marked NOT replicated, NOT calibrated.

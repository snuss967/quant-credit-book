## 1. Headline Trends

1. **US IG has crossed into an electronic majority. HY has not, and the gap between them is now the structural fact.** Coalition Greenwich has IG at 48% electronic in 2024 (52% in June 2025) and HY stuck near 30–33% for four years. MarketAxess's own estimate rose from 45% (2023) to about 60% (2025) for HG, with HY flat at 30%.
2. **The platform oligopoly is being repriced and consolidated.** Tradeweb now leads on fully electronic US credit. Trumid has grown into a third pole (about 68% YoY ADV growth in mid-2026). ICE agreed on 2026-07-29 to buy MarketAxess for $167/share (EV about $5.7bn), after MarketAxess's HG share slid from 20.4% (2023) to 17.5% (Q2-2026).
3. **Portfolio trading has moved from a 2021 novelty to standard infrastructure.** It was about 10% of IG and 8% of HY volume in 2024, reached 18% of the record $84.7bn day (2025-09-30), and covers about 10–12% of European IG. E-PT ADNV was $3.3bn in 2024, up 76%.
4. **Automation is the real story behind "electronic."** Trumid executes 88% of eligible RFQ line items fully no-touch. Tradeweb's AiEX handles more than 40% of its institutional trades. Vanguard automates about 70% of its traded volume. But buy-side desks still switch the robots to "stress parameters," or off, when markets gap.
5. **All-to-all has stabilized as a structural minority, not a takeover.** Open Trading carried 35.2% → 35.0% → 37.0% of eligible MarketAxess credit volume (2023–2025), with about 1,800 participating firms. Disclosed RFQ still carries 44% of US e-volume.
6. **ETFs have shifted from the market's shock absorber to one leg of a three-way basket market (ETF ↔ PT ↔ cash), with credit index futures emerging as a fourth.** The ETF-to-cash volume ratio fell from 23.2% (2022) to 16.6% (2024) as PT absorbed the risk transfer. ETFs re-dominate on stress days: $99bn of FI ETFs traded on 2025-04-07.
7. **Measurement itself is now contested.** Platform volumes include "electronically processed" voice. Duplicate affiliate prints inflated HG TRACE volume by up to 8% (June 2026). FINRA abandoned 1-minute TRACE reporting (Dec 2025). Since August 2026, the market leader no longer publishes monthly volumes. Any liquidity feature built on volume needs a data-provenance layer.
8. **Composite pricing has become a contested asset in its own right.** CP+ is MarketAxess's "AI-driven pricing engine." Ai-Price covers about 30k US corporates and about 1m munis. ICE's stated rationale for the deal is "where bonds are trading... and what a fair price should be." Whoever owns the composite owns the benchmark for best execution, price improvement and the PT reference price.
9. **Execution costs keep falling and non-bank liquidity keeps growing, but stress resilience is unproven.** Median EUR IG bid-ask was down 23.5% YoY in 2026, and nonbanks took about 10% of credit market-making revenue in 2024. Yet in March 2026, EUR IG bid-ask spiked 64% above its January average and took 4–13 weeks to normalize, depending on the market.

## 3. Narrative: How the Trend Evolved, Its Drivers, and Where It Heads

### 3.1 Five phases

**Phase 0, 2002–2017: transparency without electronification.** TRACE (2002) made prices public after the fact, but the trades themselves were still phone calls. MarketAxess's disclosed RFQ for small IG tickets and the early Open Trading pool were the only scaled venues.

**Phase 1, 2018–2020: the all-to-all wedge and the COVID stress test.** Quants and ETF market makers became liquidity *providers* on Open Trading. Man (2022) cites $116.5bn traded there by quant funds in Q1–Q3 2020, +150%. Dealer participation on ATSs cut customer costs by 24–32bp (NY Fed SR 938). March 2020 showed the duality of ETFs. They became the price-discovery venue while the cash market froze, but they traded at discounts and tracking errors above 200bp, with creation and redemption baskets bearing little resemblance to holdings (BIS). FIMSAC asked for a PT flag in February 2020.

**Phase 2, 2021–2022: the PT boom, then the e-trading tipping point.** Man calls 2021 "the year of portfolio trading," fueled by ETF growth and algorithmic dealer pricing. The order then reversed. In H1 2022, PT pricing widened as dealers pulled balance sheet under QT and custom ETF baskets disappeared. Electronic RFQ became the best-execution venue on cost, and Man Numeric moved more than 90% of its flow electronic. The Greenwich protocol mix shows disclosed RFQ dipping to 42% as session-based, streaming and PT protocols grew.

**Phase 3, 2023–2024: scale, flags and automation.** The TRACE PT modifier went live on 2023-05-15, and portfolio trades became observable in public data. IG e-share went from 45% to 48%. E-PT ADNV grew 76% to $3.3bn/day, with Tradeweb capturing more than 2/3. MarketAxess's Open Trading share flatlined at about 35%, while its HY share fell from 17.1% to 13.2%: PT and Trumid's session and streaming protocols were taking the incremental flow. Turnover rose to 0.44% of outstanding per day. The ETF-to-cash ratio fell because institutions could now move risk in the bonds themselves.

**Phase 4, 2025–2026: records, stress, consolidation.** ADV topped $50bn. There were record days on 2025-04-08 (12% PT), on 2025-09-30 (18% PT), and in April 2025, when $99bn of ETFs traded in a day. The robots mostly stayed on through the tariff shock, with wider parameters. The competitive map then flipped. Tradeweb took the fully electronic HG lead, and its Q2-2026 fully electronic HG share of 19.0% exceeded MarketAxess's 17.5% total HG share. Trumid grew 60–70% YoY. ICE then bought MarketAxess (2026-07-29) for its data and network, and MarketAxess stopped publishing monthly stats. AI arrived as product: CP+ is marketed as an "AI-driven pricing engine," Tradeweb launched TARA in Q2-2026, and MarketAxess released an AIQ natural-language tool. March 2026 provided the first post-tariff stress test. US IG "repriced sharply but kept trading," while EUR IG liquidity cracked, with bid-ask up 64% and trade size down 11%.

### 3.2 Drivers

1. **Dealer balance-sheet economics.** Dealers earn 26% of IG revenue from electronic channels on 48% of volume (Greenwich 2024), so they let small, liquid flow go electronic and protect voice for blocks and illiquid risk. Electronification is therefore *dealer-permitted*, not just client-demanded.
2. **Algorithmic market making plus composites.** Auto-quoters need a fair-value anchor. CP+ and Ai-Price supply it, which makes no-touch execution of small RFQs possible: Trumid executes 88% of eligible line items no-touch.
3. **ETF and index plumbing.** ETF creation and redemption, index rebalances and month-end flows produce standardized basket demand, which is exactly what PT services. The month-end PT share jumped 67% in 2021.
4. **Systematic and quant demand.** Systematic strategies run under 2% of FI (Blackstone). Tradeweb attributes the +59.5% credit-derivatives ADV in Q1-2026 to "hedge fund and systematic account activity." Quants need certainty of execution and modelable costs (Man 2023, p.4).
5. **Volatility as accelerant.** E-share jumped in 2020, 2022, April 2025 and March 2026. Stress pushes clients to electronic protocols and ETFs, even as automation parameters widen.
6. **Non-bank entrants.** Citadel Securities moved from rates into credit, pushing banks to "compete harder" (Tradeweb CEO, June 2025). Nonbanks already take about 10% of credit market-making revenue.

### 3.3 Where it heads (2026–2028)

- **IG e-share at 55–65% on the Greenwich definition by 2028; HY stays below 40% unless PT and session protocols are counted.** Barclays' ceiling estimate is about 80%.
- **PT reaches 15%+ of US IG and 18–20% of EUR IG** (Barclays forecast), and grows faster in HY (MarketAxess HY PT +93% YoY in Q2-2026). PT spread-trading in Europe (Tradeweb, Q2-2026) points to PT moving from price-based to spread-based baskets.
- **Post-close, ICE–MarketAxess will bundle evaluated pricing, indices (ICE BofA), CP+ and Open Trading.** Expect antitrust scrutiny (the outside date runs to 2028), fee pressure, and possibly reduced public disclosure of venue statistics.
- **RFQ 2.0.** FILS Europe panelists predict that RFQ "in its current form" becomes obsolete, replaced by hybrids of RFQ with pre-trade data, request-for-market (RFM), one-click RFQ, and streaming plus click-to-trade.
- **Data cleanup.** The affiliate indicator (mandatory 2027-08-02) and aggregate allocation reports change TRACE volume series *mid-sample*, and the EU and UK bond tapes go live. Liquidity models fit on 2020–2026 TRACE volume will see a structural break.
- **Listed credit.** Credit index futures block activity (Cboe) and record ETF options and volume point to a growing listed hedging layer for systematic books.

## 4. Implications for a Systematic Credit Desk

### 4.1 What to build

1. **A protocol router with fill-probability and cost models per protocol.** Route each line to disclosed RFQ, Open Trading/AllTrade, session or Swarm, Mid-X, streaming, or PT inclusion. Man (2023, p.5) describes a pre-trade filter on RFQ availability, dealer quality, RFQ frequency, completion rates and size digestibility. Formalize it as $\Pr(\text{fill}\mid x, \text{protocol})$ times the expected cost conditional on a fill, with the unfilled residual risk priced back into the optimizer.
2. **A PT construction-and-pricing engine (Ch 19).** It needs to (a) predict the dealer's basket price from composites, liquidity scores and residual covariance, (b) choose which bonds to *include* to cheapen the basket (liquid "sweeteners") and which to evict, and (c) measure the markouts of PT prints.
3. **A composite-agnostic fair-value layer.** Ingest CP+, Ai-Price, BVAL and ICE evaluated pricing plus your own TRACE-based estimate, and track each composite's bias against subsequent prints. After ICE closes, two of the four may sit under one owner.
4. **A TRACE cleaning pipeline.** Use the PT modifier (from 2023-05-15), the affiliate indicator (voluntary from 2026-08-31, mandatory 2027-08-02) and aggregate allocation reports (from 2026-06-08). Deduplicate, and tag regime breaks in volume series.
5. **Stress-mode automation parameters.** Size limits, spread-to-composite tolerances and kill switches should key off ETF discount, ETF/cash ratio and bid-ask percentile, mirroring Vanguard's "fair-weather vs. stress parameters."
6. **ETF and futures hedge overlays.** Hedge with HYG/JNK/LQD, credit index futures (IBHY/IBIG) and CDX, choosing by DTS-matched cost and basis risk (Ch 8, Ch 20).

### 4.2 What to trade

- **Liquidity provision on all-to-all.** Quant books can earn the Open Trading liquidity-provider price improvement, which MarketAxess estimated at $171.6m in 2025, measured against CP+. The risk is adverse selection by better-informed takers; see the RSSG note below.
- **Month-end, index-rebalance and ETF-basket flow.** These are predictable demand for standardized baskets. PT and ETF creation and redemption concentrate it, and the month-end effect was measurable in 2021–2022.
- **ETF ↔ cash ↔ futures basis in stress.** Discounts were large in March 2020 and April 2025 ($99bn ETF day). The arbitrage is capital-intensive and needs AP or LP access.
- **Evicted and orphan bonds.** Bonds that PT dealers and ETF redemption baskets push out (redemption baskets are shorter and less liquid, per BIS) are a recurring supply of mispriced paper.
- **The new-issue window.** Trumid handled 42% of secondary trading in the first two days after issuance (Sept 2025). Systematic new-issue-concession strategies now execute electronically.

### 4.3 What to worry about

- **Fair-weather liquidity.** Platforms and ELPs "help mainly in normal markets." EUR IG's March 2026 bid-ask spike and its 13-week EUR HY recovery mean backtests using 2023–2025 costs understate stress costs. Run LVaR with a 3–5× cost multiplier (Ch 28).
- **Composite reflexivity.** When auto-quoters, price-improvement benchmarks and PT reference prices all key off the same composite, composite error becomes correlated execution error.
- **Venue concentration and data access.** ICE–MarketAxess could change fees, data licensing and disclosure. MarketAxess's monthly data has already gone dark, and Tradeweb's data distribution runs through an LSEG license to October 2028.
- **Measurement error in "liquidity."** Duplicate prints (up to 8% of HG volume), "electronically processed" volume and estimated electronic shares (Trumid at 60–71%) bias volume-based liquidity scores.
- **Crowding of systematic execution.** Many quants routing the same liquid names through the same auto-ex protocols at the same times (month-end, lunch, August) shrinks the "liquidity sweet spot."

### 4.4 For the RSSG-to-quant reader specifically

The electronic market is the market for *boring* risk. Your edge sits in the 20–50% that is not electronic: HY blocks, stressed names, LMT candidates, and anything trading "name by name" in a gap. Three concrete translations:

1. **PT "mystery box" toxicity.** A 150-line HY basket can hide two or three LMT-exposed names. The RSSG skill set (reading credit agreements, spotting ad hoc group formation) becomes a *basket-screening feature*: flag lines with a high Priming Risk Score (Ch 13) before the desk prices the basket.
2. **All-to-all adverse selection.** On Open Trading you are anonymous liquidity to people who may know about an impending exchange. Ines's question, "who at this table knows more than you?", becomes a counterparty-type × RFQ-timing × markout model (Ch 3).
3. **When the robots switch off.** In stress, flow reverts to dealers and voice, and that is where RSSG-style capital-structure judgment is paid. Build the playbook for the regime switch, not just the steady state.

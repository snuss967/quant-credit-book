## Method and verification notes

- **Dates.** Taken from the item's own page where it prints one (Omny `PublishedUtc`, article metadata, seminar listing) and otherwise from the publisher's RSS feed `pubDate`, fetched 2026-10-18. Transistor (9fin, Octus) episode pages render their date by script, so those dates are the feed dates. An undated page is marked "(page undated)".
- **Fetch.** Every row's URL returned HTTP 200 on 2026-10-18 when fetched with curl or WebFetch. Hosts that returned 403 to scripted fetches (Bloomberg.com, Alpha Architect, FA Magazine, Gabelli, some university pages) are not used as rows.
- **Not in a row.** Anything I could see only in a search snippet, anything behind a 403, and anything whose date I could not read is in "Unverified leads".
- **Numbers.** Where a "why" line quotes a figure it is the item's own claim, dated, and is not independently audited here; Ch 13 and Ch 14 writers should cite the primary study, not the podcast.
- **Section anchors** (`§8.6` etc.) are the headings in `book_src/` as of 2026-10-18.

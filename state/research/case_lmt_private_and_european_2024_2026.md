- IFR Awards 2025 'EMEA Restructuring: Altice France's EUR24bn restructuring' (fetched): debt price fell ~20 cents on the dollar during the March 2024 investor call at which Drahi outlined plans to cut debt (days after Altice Media sale agreed, which had given bondholders hope proceeds would repay them); a creditor cooperation agreement formed the evening of that call, reportedly among the first of its kind in Europe, growing to ~200 institutions holding ~EUR19bn of opco-secured debt; named participants: Elliott, Anchorage, Sona, Sculptor, plus Arini (major holder of the ~EUR4bn holdco debt); Pimco and BlackRock major holders; advisers: Lazard (Drahi/company), Rothschild (creditors; Arnaud Joubert), Houlihan Lokey (Arini); negotiations began Nov 2024 and reached agreement in ~3 months; creditors got 45% of equity (Drahi's opening offer was a haircut with little equity); maturities extended up to five years; Rothschild argued French law would protect creditors against US-style LMEs.

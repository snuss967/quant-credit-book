### RAW: Pluralsight facts batch 1
- Vista take-private announced Dec 2020 (Pluralsight press release cited by L&G), closed Apr 2021, ~$3.5bn valuation; financed partly by a $1.175bn recurring-revenue loan plus $100mm revolver (Goodwin alert 12 Aug 2024 citing FT); L&G says "over $1.5bn of private credit debt". [S: goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2024/08/alerts-privateequity-ma-pluralsight-change-of-control-transaction; blog.landg.com/categories/markets-and-economics/pluralsight--a-big-test-for-private-credit]
- KBRA 5 Jun 2024: BDC first-lien exposure ~$375mm FMV at 1Q24 of ~$700mm total BDC exposure ($736.79mm at 4Q23, $698.08mm at 1Q24); floating S+800 typical (BlackRock DLC S+815); maturity mostly April 2027 (Golub 5 and 7 Apr 2027). KBRA-rated BDCs: five hold <1% of investments, two <2.5%. Structured credit 2.3-3.9% of par of total assets; private funds <1%. KBRA called IP-to-subsidiary financing "reasonable and allowable stopgap measures", expected no rating impact. [S: kbra.com/publications/mpftfNbZ/...]
- KBRA mark table (cents on the dollar, 4Q23 -> 1Q24): Blue Owl entities 95.7-96.8 -> 83.4-83.5 (~13 pt drop; BOCC FV ~$83.0mm, BOTF $133.2mm); Goldman Sachs BDC 95.00 -> 88.0 (a separate 72.71 tranche marked up to 88.0); Franklin BSP Capital Corp 92.34-94.14 -> 87.8; Ares Capital 88.98 -> 84.9 (FV $90.2mm); BlackRock TCP 97.50 -> 90.2; Oaktree OCSL 96.20 -> 90.1 ($60.6mm) plus Strategic Credit Fund $23.6mm; Golub (unitranche) 99.00 -> 97.0 ($23.0mm). Range at 1Q24: 83.4 to 97.0 = 13.6 pts same-loan dispersion. [S: KBRA 5 Jun 2024]
- L&G (19 Sep 2024): by Q2 2024 "all lenders marked down the Pluralsight loan to 45-50% of its original value" (CHECK against 10-Qs); exposure 0.5-1.5% of assets for affected BDCs; named creditors Blue Owl, Ares, Goldman Sachs, Franklin BSP, BlackRock, Oaktree, Golub. [S: blog.landg.com]
- Drop-down: early summer 2024 Pluralsight transferred certain IP to a new wholly owned restricted subsidiary; $50mm Vista loan or preferred equity into that sub (reports differ) funded an upcoming interest payment to the lenders; Vista reportedly repaid $50mm in the later deal; permitted under credit agreement per KBRA; creditors reportedly alleged improper valuation of the assets moved; third-party valuation firm. FT: "gave Vista first priority to be repaid on the extra capital it was putting into the business". Goodwin: "one of, if not, the first sponsor funded LME" of a private-credit backed company. [S: Goodwin 12 Aug 2024; KBRA 5 Jun 2024]
- Bloomberg 3 Jun 2024: Vista in talks to hand Pluralsight to creditors (investing.com syndication). Lenders take control: Bloomberg 21-22 Aug 2024 via PE Wire 23 Aug 2024: Blue Owl- and Ares-led group; ~$275mm fresh capital ($125mm already funded that week); ~$1.2bn debt converted to equity; lenders 85%, management 15%; Vista and co-investors lose ~$4bn. Goodwin's 12 Aug alert: lenders to take 100%, funded debt down $1.3bn, $250mm new capital (numbers differ - reports were pre-signing). [S: privateequitywire.co.uk article; BNN Bloomberg 22 Aug 2024; Goodwin]
- Ares Capital Q2 2024 (reported 30 Jul 2024): $48mm unrealized losses tied partly to Pluralsight; Q2 GAAP EPS $0.52 vs $0.61 yoy; CEO Kipp deVeer on stepping in and owning a company. [S: commercialobserver.com/?p=484471]
- Feb 2026: Oaktree Specialty Lending (earnings call) and Ares Capital (filings) marked Pluralsight equity to zero ~18 months after takeover. Goldman Sachs BDC Q4 2025 release: 1L Pluralsight Inc. placed on non-accrual. [S: news.bgov.com 'Oaktree, Ares Write Off Pluralsight Equity'; barchart GS BDC Dec 31 2025 release]

### RAW: Pluralsight facts batch 2
- Reuters (Milana Vinn), 2 Jul 2024: Vista in talks to cede control; Vista bought Pluralsight for $3.9bn in 2021 (Goodwin/L&G say ~$3.5bn; CHECK basis: enterprise value vs headline), merged with A Cloud Guru (bought for $1.6bn); >$1.5bn non-bank debt placed ~3 yrs earlier ("recurring revenue term loan"); pre-acquisition net debt ~$500mm. Vista fund docs: equity valued $2.3bn in Q1 2023, $1.5bn in Q4 2023, zero in Q1 2024 (update dated 28 May 2024); Q1 2023 Vista asked lenders to loosen covenants and injected $75mm equity; Q3 2023 revenue declines reported to fund investors; 15 Apr 2024 update warned of "liquidity challenges" early Q2 2024; Vista lent $162.5mm via a subscription-line facility; CEO Aaron Skonnard replaced by Chris Walters Apr 2024, 2024 renewal rate projected in the low 70s percent. Causes cited: IT staff cuts by customers, higher rates, free competitor learning products in Q1 2024. Vista said company "could not service its debt" / marked equity to zero on "liquidity constraints due to the debt structure". KBRA: "may serve as an important bellwether for the industry". [S: finance.yahoo.com/news/exclusive-vista-equity-talks-hand-183356419.html (Reuters), fetched this session]
- 30 Jun 2024 BDC marks (SECONDARY, via search-engine summary; CHECK against 10-Q): Blue Owl's $99.5mm S+800 first-lien loan to Pluralsight at FV $46.7mm (46.9c); Ares Capital's $106.2mm piece at FV $51.0mm (48.0c). Consistent with L&G "45-50% of original value".
- Note the mark-path: Q4'23 ~89-99 -> Q1'24 83.4-97.0 -> Q2'24 ~47-48 (Blue Owl, Ares) -> equity-for-debt Aug 2024.

### RAW: Pluralsight facts batch 4 (PRIMARY: ARCC 10-Q for quarter ended 30 Sep 2024, sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1287750/000128775024000054/arcc-20240930.htm, downloaded and parsed this session)

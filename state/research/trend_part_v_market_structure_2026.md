| definition | FINRA's own monthly TRACE volume file reports "Par Value" as the uncapped sum of reported trade sizes in $mm; counts S1 (sell-side) reports only for CORP/CONV/ELN/AGCY/ABS; interdealer counts the sell side only while dealer-to-customer counts both buys and sells; Rule 144A included; published on the third business day after month-end; monthly and average-daily totals only, no daily series. So Greenwich's "record day" (market-maker/dealer/platform survey data) and a TRACE par-volume day are different constructs. | FINRA, About TRACE Monthly Volume Reports, https://www.finra.org/finra-data/browse-catalog/trace-volume-reports/about-trace-monthly-volume-reports |

# TREND Dossier: Part V Market Structure as of H2 2026

*Researcher dossier for Ch 2, Ch 18b, Ch 22, App C (feeds per `state/research_queue_ed2.json`, slug `part_v_market_structure_2026`). Compiled 2026-10-18. Scope: non-bank dealer share of IG/HY TRACE volume 2022-2026; stress-day RFQ statistics (2-9 Apr 2025, 27-30 Mar 2026); record-day definitions ($89.7bn 8 Apr 2025 vs $84.7bn 30 Sep 2025); ICE-MarketAxess terms and the end of monthly volume publication; duplicate affiliate prints; rule status (dealer-rule vacatur, Treasury clearing, Basel III endgame re-proposal, SBSR, EU/UK tapes, T+1 2027); CDS clearing shares.*

## 1. Headline trends

1. **The denominator is moving.** Record days, platform shares and "fully electronic" volumes now rest on shifting definitions: Greenwich restated its 30 Sep 2025 record from $89bn to $84.7bn; FINRA's affiliate-principal indicator (voluntary from 2026-08-31, mandatory 2027-08-02) removes duplicate prints, which FINRA sized at up to 5.7% of 2025 transactions; MarketAxess stopped monthly volume releases on 2026-07-30; Tradeweb now footnotes a series break. Any 2026 share or volume series needs a break dummy.
2. **Platforms are consolidating while fees fall.** ICE will buy MarketAxess for $167 a share (close H1-2027; HSR clock now runs to 2026-10-29). MarketAxess's US HG share slid from 19.2% to 17.7% (1H-2026) as portfolio trading (21.9% of its US credit volume in March 2026) and Tradeweb/Trumid took share; MarketAxess credit fees per million fell to $129 in Q2 from $138.
3. **Stress liquidity is two-speed, and only one speed is measured.** In April 2025 US HG prices per RFQ halved (13.2 to 6.6 on 9 April) and recovered to 11.3 within a week; in March 2026 EUR IG bid-ask rose 88% off its February low. No platform publishes a March 2026 response or hit-rate series, while buy-side desks say electronic liquidity providers "help more in quiet markets."
4. **Non-bank balance sheet is large but not publicly measurable.** Jane Street issued $14.6bn of BB-rated secured notes; HRT reportedly earned $6.4bn in Q1-2026; Citadel claims 14.2% of IG block risk transfer (self-reported). TRACE shows flat top-5 concentration (33.8% of par) but 42% more firms per day in $25mm+ trades (2023 to 2025).
5. **Index credit is a cleared product; single-name is not.** ISDA H1-2026: 80.2% of index notional cleared (CDX IG 99.3%), against 63.7% for single-name CDS.
6. **Regulation is delay and relief, not new burden.** The dealer rule is dead; Basel III endgame is re-proposed (comments closed 2026-06-18, not final); SBSR relief runs to 2029; Treasury clearing holds at 2026-12-31 and 2027-06-30; EU/UK/CH T+1 is 2027-10-11; the UK bond tape is live, the EU bond tape is not yet authorised.

## 3. Narrative: drivers and the 2026-2028 outlook

**What the record-day puzzle teaches.** Greenwich's four record-day entries cannot be one series. MarketAxess puts the 8 April 2025 peak at $55.9bn for US HG alone; adding even a HY day at 1.5x April's $15.7bn ADV gives about $79bn, still $10bn short of Greenwich's $89.7bn. The 30 Sep 2025 record went from $89bn to $84.7bn (-4.8%) with no stated reason, and an $84.7bn record cannot coexist with an unrestated $92bn on 30 April. One untested hypothesis is that the restatement removes duplicate affiliate prints, which FINRA sizes at up to 5.7% of 2025 transactions; the match in magnitude is suggestive, not proof. The rule for the book: quote a record only with its publisher, definition and date, and prefer FINRA's monthly par file.

**Driver 1, balance sheet.** The Basel re-proposal lowers aggregate CET1 requirements (-4.8% combined) yet raises trading-related RWA 30.9% and market-risk RWA 20.5% for the nine largest holding companies, so the marginal dollar of bond inventory is not cheaper at a bank. With the dealer rule vacated, principal trading firms stay outside dealer registration and fund themselves in the high-yield market (Jane Street Ba1/BB). The structural direction is unchanged: more risk warehoused by non-banks. What TRACE can show is thin: top-5 concentration is flat, but the daily count of block-size reporters rose 42% in two years.

**Driver 2, protocol substitution.** Portfolio trading and sessions displace line-item RFQ, which lowers fees per million and breaks "share" metrics. MarketAxess's own board model assumes fees "reflect a higher decline." The platform owner is being repriced at 10.6x EBITDA because its volume is real but its pricing power is not.

**Driver 3, vertical integration.** ICE would own the MarketAxess data and composite pricing alongside its reference data, indices and, since 2026-01-30, a Treasury clearing house. The antitrust asymmetry is the signal: ICE's regulatory break fee ($327.4m, about 5.5% of equity value) is 2.2x MarketAxess's own ($148.8m, 2.5%), and the HSR filing was withdrawn and refiled to give DOJ time. Price discovery, hedging (index CDS is 99% cleared) and settlement are converging on a few owners.

**Driver 4, measurement and tapes.** The UK tape (live 2026-06-22 at 98% coverage) and the EU tape (selected, unauthorised) will eventually give a regulatory consolidated price, but ETS's CEO concedes the tape is "simply a cost to contributors with no commercial benefit," and The DESK attributes January 2026's jump in EUR trade sizes to dealers adapting to the new European disclosure regime.

**Stress evidence, reconciled.** April 2025 (response count 13.2 to 6.6, algos about 80% of liquidity, recovery in days) and March 2026 (EUR IG +88%, 4 to 14 weeks to normalise per the earlier dossier) agree with the Bank of Canada model: non-banks improve normal-time liquidity more than stress liquidity. April 2025 was a one-day air pocket; March 2026 was a slower energy-price grind visible as wider spreads and smaller sizes. Whether quotes went missing in March 2026 is unmeasured in public data (one buy-side panelist reports "days where you wouldn't have the opportunity to trade at all", undated).

**2026-2028 calendar.** 2026-10-29: MarketAxess vote and HSR expiry. 2026-12-31: Treasury cash clearing. H1-2027: ICE close target (outside date 2027-07-29, extendable to 2028-07-29). 2027-06-30: repo clearing. 2027-08-02: mandatory affiliate indicator. 2027-10-11: EU/UK/CH T+1. Undated: Basel final rule (no effective date proposed), EU bond tape go-live. 2029-11-05: SBSR relief ends.

**Scenarios (judgment, not model output).** (A, about 60%) Orderly consolidation: DOJ clears with remedies by mid-2027, fees keep compressing, PT passes a quarter of US credit volume. (B, about 30%) Antitrust friction: second request, extension into 2028, share continues to leak to Tradeweb and Trumid. (C, about 10%) Deal breaks or a volatility event in 2027 exposes response counts; the first test of non-bank stress liquidity at current size.

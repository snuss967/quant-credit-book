| definition | FINRA's own monthly TRACE volume file reports "Par Value" as the uncapped sum of reported trade sizes in $mm; counts S1 (sell-side) reports only for CORP/CONV/ELN/AGCY/ABS; interdealer counts the sell side only while dealer-to-customer counts both buys and sells; Rule 144A included; published on the third business day after month-end; monthly and average-daily totals only, no daily series.

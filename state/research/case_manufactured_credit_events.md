Reading the table. Where open interest is to buy, the final price sits above the midpoint: +6.4 points (Codere), +24.25 (Sears), +2.75 (Windstream). The auction terms cap only the other direction: with net buying, offers below the midpoint minus the Cap Amount (1.00% for Codere, Sears and Windstream) are treated as at that floor; nothing caps the upside [S16]. In Sears, dealers' two-way markets ran from 45/50 (Citi) to 59/64 (Morgan Stanley), a spread of about 14 points between the lowest and highest midpoints, against about 3 for Windstream (24/27 to 27/30); stage 1 was widely dispersed before stage 2 added 24 points. A thin auction is cheap to move: 8 to 12 dealers took part, and later auctions had 4 to 7 [S17].

**Recoveries.** A Sears final price of 79.875 pays protection buyers 20.1 per 100, against 44.4 at the midpoint; on $100mm of notional that is $24.25mm moving from buyers to sellers inside one afternoon. A Windstream price of 29.5 paid 70.5. The anonymous Sears submission said the scarce unsecured deliverables traded "40 points above" second-lien notes that were not deliverable, and that free trading was under $1mm a day [S12].

## 5. Legal and structural mechanics

**Failure to Pay.** A missed payment of at least the Payment Requirement ($1mm, North American), after the Grace Period, under any Obligation. If the instrument has no grace period for principal, a three-Grace-Period-Business-Day period is deemed, and without Grace Period Extension it ends no later than the scheduled termination date (iHeart: event date 20 Dec 2016) [S26]. The DC looks only at public information and applies the text: in iHeart the trustee's notice said the affiliate holder "did not waive its underlying right to be repaid" and no supplemental indenture moved the maturity, so the due date stood; the DC did not ask why the issuer withheld [S26, S22]. There is no cross-default or acceleration requirement, which is why a $1.04mm payment can be a credit event.

**The clause that made it legal.** The Statement of Information to the DC (13 Mar 2018) cites s.11.1(b)(iii) of the 2014 Definitions (s.9.1(b)(iii) of the 2003) and *Good Hill Master Fund v. Deutsche Bank*, 146 A.D.3d 632 (1st Dep't 2017), which held a CDS party could act "without regard to the existence of the swap or any adverse effect it might have on [the counterparty]'s position". Swain's opinion never reached the merits; Solus's claims ended in a no-payment, no-admission dismissal [S3, S7].

**Deliverables.** Payout is 1 minus the price of the cheapest deliverable. For North American corporates the 2014 Definitions' characteristics are: Bond or Loan; Not Subordinated; Specified Currency; Assignable Loan; Consent Required Loan; Transferable; Maximum Maturity 30 years; Not Bearer. The Updated 2003 Definitions add Not Contingent, which the 2014 text replaced with the Outstanding Principal Balance machinery [S14]. Three clauses decided these cases. *Consent Required Loan* (s.3.14(b)(ii), "capable of being assigned or novated with the consent of the Reference Entity ... or any agent"): the Sears panel held an Eligible-Assignee clause that narrowed "but not eliminat[ed]" the buyer pool did not defeat it [S10]. *Borrowed Money* (s.3.13(a)(ii)) and *Transferable*: the Sears challenge argued intercompany paper might not be money borrowed, could not be delivered out of a bankruptcy estate, and might be recharacterised as equity; the DC excluded undocumented programs and admitted the documented series [S9, S12]. *Outstanding Principal Balance* (s.3.8): Windstream's challengers argued the New Notes had no determinable balance and the Aurelius notes might be equitably subordinated to a zero balance; the DC rejected the challenge 15 to 0 and found "insufficient basis" for less than par [S13].

**Auction rules.** The DC holds an auction if at least 300 transactions on auction settlement, confirmed through DTCC and with at least five dealer-members as parties, are outstanding (the Relevant Transaction 300/5 Criteria); otherwise the DC decides by a majority. Net physical interest on one side is matched against opposite limit orders, and the Cap Amount is one-sided as described in §3 [S19, S16]. Where the DC calls "no auction" under s.6.11 of the 2014 Definitions, buyers fall back to physical settlement with a notice cut-off (1 Aug 2025 for the Altice Holding) [S18].

**NTCE (15 Jul 2019).** If elected, (a) a Failure to Pay does not count unless it "directly or indirectly either result[s] from, or result[s] in, a deterioration in the creditworthiness or financial condition of the Reference Entity"; (b) Outstanding Principal Balance is cut to a straight-line interpolation if a bond is issued below 95% of redemption value, and in an exchange the issue price is deemed to be the tendered bonds' balance, allocated across the new bonds by the Calculation Agent. Exhibit F lists indicators that the requirement is not met: an arrangement with "an essential purpose" of creating a CDS benefit; new debt "likely to be the cheapest-to-deliver" or material extra deliverables; no acceleration; ample liquidity and no technical cause; prompt cure; debt held by affiliates. And indicators that it is met: prior distress, restructuring advisers, a court-supervised process, widely held debt, inability to refinance, other debt accelerating or bankruptcy. A bona fide standstill, or a payment skipped to win hedged creditors into a restructuring that avoids bankruptcy, "rarely" fails the test [S20]. Because it works through Failure to Pay, it does not reach orphaning, non-defaults, or squeezes by deliverable holders.

## 7. Outcome and later rulings

- **Hovnanian:** no credit event; interest cured 30 May 2018; GSO's financing stood "unchanged"; Solus recovered nothing and was paid nothing [S6, S7]. No court ever ruled that inducing a default is or is not manipulation. As of Dec 2019 no regulator had brought a case (Sidley, secondary) [S27].
- **Sears:** the DC admitted the intercompany notes (12 to 3), the External Review Panel (21 Dec 2018) admitted the loans, expressly "not [a] situation such as might arise through a 'manufactured' set of facts", and reserved the point for restrictions "so commercially unreasonable" as to block assignment. The anonymous request to delay the auction was not granted; it ran on 17 Jan and cleared 24 points above the midpoint [S9, S12, S17].
- **Windstream:** protection that expired between the Dec 2017 acceleration and the Feb 2019 ruling never paid; a request to backdate was rejected; contracts alive on 25 Feb 2019 paid 70.5 [S13, S17].
- **Altice France:** Holding contracts settled only physically; OpCo contracts auctioned at 87.75 [S17, S18].
- **The rule fix** (NTCE, 2019-2020) is contractual and elective. Governance changes since: S&P Global Market Intelligence named DC Administrator in June 2026 [S19].

(3) New Exhibit F guidance: indicators the requirement is NOT satisfied (1.10): (a) non-payment arises directly from an arrangement where "an essential purpose" is to create a benefit under a CDS to buyer or seller; (b) the arrangement has the entity issue a new debt "likely to be the cheapest-to-deliver" or "a material amount of additional debt obligations that would constitute Deliverable Obligations"; (c) non-payment did not cause other debt to be accelerated or capable of acceleration; (d) entity had access to sufficient liquidity and no technical/administrative/operational cause; (e) non-payment promptly cured after grace period; (f) related only to debt held by affiliates or others unlikely to accelerate. Indicators it IS satisfied (1.11): prior announcement of distress/restructuring or forbearance; restructuring advisers appointed; court- or insolvency-official-overseen creditor process; debt held by a number of parties; inability to refinance; payment date was an original scheduled date; other debt accelerated/unpaid or Bankruptcy follows. Paras 1.13-1.14: bona fide forbearance/standstill "would rarely" fail the test; a failure to pay agreed with hedged creditors to make a bona fide restructuring succeed, where otherwise bankruptcy is likely, "should generally be considered to have the essential purpose of facilitating such restructuring".

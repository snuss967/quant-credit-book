13. **Rule 6730 correction.** `life_quant_shop_daily_life.md` §2.3 says later reports are "as/of". FINRA Rule 6730 designates a report that misses the 15 minutes "late", and reserves "as/of" for reports made on a later date (trades before 8:00 a.m. ET are reported after the system opens the same day; trades in the last 15 minutes before the 6:30 p.m. close, or after it, go T+1 as "as/of"). Section 18.8 now says so. Ch 1-B line 175 ("one late report now flagged as/of") may need the same check.

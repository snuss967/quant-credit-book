| 2026-06-11 | NAIC Life RBC Working Group adopted new collateral-loan charges (effective RBC as of 31 Dec 2027): base 30% for LP/LLC/JV interests and 45% for residual tranches, falling with overcollateralization: OC 111-143% (LTV ~80%) 20% haircut, so 24% / 36%; OC 143-200% 40% haircut, so 18% / 27%; OC at or above 200% 50% haircut, so 15% / 22.5%. Haircut capped at 50%; reduced charges need independent fair-value verification. Replaces the uniform 6.8% (still the charge for "all other" collateral). Original proposal was a uniform 20% haircut; an ACLI alternative produced the OC-based grid. | Sidley (Jul 2026) |

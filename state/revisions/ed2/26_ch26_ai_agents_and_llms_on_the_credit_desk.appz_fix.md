- Note: the §26.6 Full Derivation says "retry a rejected step up to $m$ times", but the formula $a_R(1-r^m)/(1-r)$ and every number after it treat $m$ as total attempts ($m=1$ gives the first-try coverage 0.83; §26.12 says "one try to three"). Read literally, $m=2$ would be three attempts.

With $m$ = retries (m+1 attempts), m=2 would give (0.9479, 0.9745), not the exercise's 0.953 / 0.944.

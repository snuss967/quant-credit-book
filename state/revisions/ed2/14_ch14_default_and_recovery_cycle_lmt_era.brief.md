## 1. The spine

The reader should finish the cold open holding one uneasy question: **"This loan already defaulted and it is still trading. What is it, and what am I being paid for?"** The 2015 answer was a point: PD times LGD, one event, one recovery table. The chapter's answer is that the object is now a *joint law* of two clocks and two humps, and that the single most important input to it, which side of the room you were on, is not on the term sheet. The chapter earns that answer in order: the definition fork (14.1), the two taps draining one bucket (14.2), the parole clock (14.3), the two humps (14.4), the cartel that controls which hump you land in (14.5), the courts that reprice the humps on a date (14.6), a filter that tells you which world you are in (14.7), three real clocks (14.8), and the trades (14.9). The emotional throughline is Theo discovering that his USB-stick 13-week model, the thing he thought he had to leave behind, is literally the covariate that moves the re-default scale.

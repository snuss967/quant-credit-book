## 1. The spine

The cold open already ends on the right sentence: Theo bid 85½, was covered at 85¾, and "has no idea whether that was a good outcome." Edition 2 should make the reader *feel* that not-knowing before the chapter names it. The question is: **was 85½ a good bid, and how could anyone ever know?** Right now the answer arrives as a syllabus ("by the end of this chapter you will be able to..."). Keep that paragraph but move it after a new beat: Theo walks home Friday night replaying the hand and discovers he cannot say what "good" would even mean. Good because the bond went down? He would have been right for the wrong reason. Good because he was covered? That is being second. The chapter then answers in order: a bid is a posterior, not a markup (§3.2); you learn whether it was good only from the hand history, beta-stripped (§3.3); the posterior moves when the 8-K lands (§3.4); what you know that moves it faster than the tape is the Ledger (§3.5); and the most expensive thing someone across the table can know is an LMT (§3.6, §3.7). The Desk Debrief already closes the loop ("Second is not a strategy"). The two long scenes carry the two halves: SVB Financial's holdco notes in March 2023 (the 8-K scene, §3.4) and Serta in June 2020 (the LMT scene, §3.7). The reviewers' asks fold in: the cleansing mechanism and restricted list (RSSG partner #9, desk head #10) in §3.6.3; the hand-history replay figure (engagement review); and the Ch 25 → Ch 26 reference fix at §3.5.

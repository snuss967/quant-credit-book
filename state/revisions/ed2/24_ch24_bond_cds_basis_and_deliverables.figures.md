3. **ch24_ctd_deliverable_prices** (Figure 24.3, section 24.5.1, after the "Hovnanian blueprint" paragraph). 5. **ch24_successor_cliffs** (Figure 24.5, section 24.8.1, after the "With m Successors" paragraph).

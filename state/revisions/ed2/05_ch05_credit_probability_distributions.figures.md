## Notes for the editor

- Discrepancy in the §5.1.4 table (not changed, flagged for the verifier): the table's Gaussian column is N(7%, 4.1%) (95% VaR +0.3%, 99% VaR -2.5%, 99% ES -3.9% all check at mean 7%), but its "1 in 29,000" for losing 15 points reproduces only with a zero-mean N(0, 4.1%) and a 15/92 = 16.3% loss (z = 3.98); at mean 7% the chance is about 1 in 150 million.

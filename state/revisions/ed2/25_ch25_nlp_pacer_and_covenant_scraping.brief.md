## 1. The spine

The feeling the cold open must leave: **the people who wrote the sentence already know the number, and you are the last player at the table to look at your own cards.** Ines says a version of this in the current cold open; Edition 2 makes the reader feel it on a real day. The chapter then answers it in one movement: a credit agreement is a program (definitions are variables, covenants are assertions), a basket is a function that returns dollars, leakage is a flow through baskets (J.Crew), an amendment is a change in the capacity vector rather than in the tokens (Windstream), the docket is an append-only log whose marks and sequences carry the trade (Serta), and every number must point back to its span because classifier error is an exchange rate on alpha. The three real events are the three layers of §25.1.1: contract (J.Crew), amendment and court (Windstream), docket (Serta). Say that once, in the bridge after the cold open, and let the reader notice it again at the Debrief.

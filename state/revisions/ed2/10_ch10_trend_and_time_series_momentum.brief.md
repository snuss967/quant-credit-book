Make the reader feel it through Ines's chip. Mags says it looks like April 2025, like summer 2007, and like nothing at all. The reader should close the cold open holding three possible futures and no rule for choosing. The chapter then answers in the only honest way it can: not "which one is it" but "what is the position worth under each, and how does the engine keep the bet small until the market tells you". Each tool is a piece of that answer: the carry hurdle says how far spreads must go before a return-based rule even calls this a trend; DTS scaling says the position is already shrinking as spreads widen, signal or no signal; the two-coefficient blend says carry and value are pulling the other way exactly now; the equity lead says where to look for the next move before the bond prints it; the smile and the whipsaw say what the payoff looks like if this is 2007 (slow, convex, paid) or April 2025 (a gap, a V, a loss); the jump correction says why none of this applies to the single name Mags is about to buy. The chapter must come back to 4:02 p.m. in the Debrief and let Theo write his card: what size, at what signal speed, and what would make him cover.

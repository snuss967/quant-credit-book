- **Figure 8.1, ch08_hedge_ratio_by_rating.png** (8.2, after the "Read the table like a credit agreement" paragraph): H(s) = beta + gamma*s lines for Baa, Ba, B and Caa, each ending at the table's H at the maximum sample OAS; the 700 bp overlap test shows Baa at 0.71 against +0.06, -0.06, -0.07 for Ba, B, Caa (the Baa 0.71 reproduces Ben Dor p.207). - **Figure 8.2, ch08_recovery_route.png** (8.3, after the H^rec = 0.52 paragraph): left, screen duration 1/yield against true duration 1/(r+lambda) of the perpetuity (7.7 vs 4.0 years at lambda = 21%); right, H^rec = c/(c+lambda R) for R = 20, 40, 60%, with 0.52 marked. Data: Illustrative, the section 8.3 model with c = 9%, R = 40%, r = 4% (r only enters the left panel; the chapter does not state r).
- **Figure 8.3, ch08_stale_marks_beta.png** (8.5, after the Scholes-Williams paragraph): left, 60 days of cumulative true versus stale-marked return; right, OLS 0.494, Scholes-Williams 0.812 and Dimson 1.005, each stacked by the lag that supplied it. Data: Illustrative, executes Listings 8.1-8.4 of Appendix F in one namespace (seed 8), so the bars are the numbers section 8.5 and 8.9 print.
- **Figure 8.4, ch08_rolling_hedge_ratio.png** (8.7 Case Study C, after the "indictment of trailing windows" paragraph): rolling 60-day H = 1 + sbar*rho*sigma_s/sigma_y for IG, HY and CCC, January 2024 to 15 October 2026, with the 2 April 2025 event line. Data: REAL, FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2, BAMLH0A3HYC, BAMLC0A0CM, DGS10 pulled 2026-10-18 (794 daily rows from 2023-10-17). Raw ICE series are cached in .scratch/ (git-ignored; ICE terms bar redistribution); only the derived rolling estimate is stored, in tools/figures/data/ch08_rolling_hedge_ratio.csv.

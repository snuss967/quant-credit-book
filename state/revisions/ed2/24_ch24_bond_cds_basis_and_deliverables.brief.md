## 1. The spine

The cold open already ends on the right line, Kaz's "which one of them is lying?" The reader should feel, by the end of the opening scene, that the bond and the swap are two witnesses to the same company who have been shown different documents. The chapter's answer, built section by section, is that neither is lying: the swap is a contract whose payoff is set by a committee, an auction, and the cheapest thing anyone can find to deliver, while the bond is a claim on cash flows. "Basis" is the price of whichever of the six assumptions A1–A6 is currently broken. The chapter ends where it starts, with Theo's question "what do the Definitions say?" turning out to be the quant question too. Four moments carry the argument: Ines's "against what?" (§24.3), March 2020's haircut call (§24.4), the Sears afternoon when a thin auction moved 24 points (§24.6), and the Hovnanian risk meeting where a hazard model says "never" and an indenture says "1 May" (§24.8).

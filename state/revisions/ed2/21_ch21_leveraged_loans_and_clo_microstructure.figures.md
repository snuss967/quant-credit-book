# Ch 21 figures log (Edition 2, U4)

Script: tools/figures/ch21.py (run from the repo root: `python3 tools/figures/ch21.py`). No data cache and no download: every number is either cited in the chapter text or generated from a model stated in the chapter. No brief existed for this chapter, so the figures were chosen from the headings (one per major mechanism: settlement, CLO arbitrage, OC cliff and cure, the loan's embedded call, the redemption queue).

Plan (5 figures): ch21_settlement_clock (s21.2.2), ch21_clo_arbitrage (s21.3.1), ch21_ccc_cliff_and_cure (s21.4.2), ch21_loan_short_call (s21.5.1), ch21_buffer_queue (s21.6.1).

## Embedded figures

1. **Figure 21.1, ch21_settlement_clock.png** (s21.2.2, after the "forced repo" paragraph that ends "both sit idle"). Dot plot in business days: bonds T+3 -> T+2 -> T+1; loan par convention T+10 (2004) -> T+7, with the T+5 paperwork deadline; observed loan medians T+12 -> T+9 after the September 2016 protocol and the roughly T+20 ten-year average. Background shades the dead zone (T to T+7) and the delayed-compensation window (after T+7). Data: Moyer p.322; LSTA delayed-comp protocol medians via Markets Media (2 Nov 2016); the 20-day average is read off an LSTA chart via search, which the chapter itself flags as approximate (the caption says so).
2. **Figure 21.2, ch21_clo_arbitrage.png** (s21.3.1, after the "Compare two vintages" paragraph). Left: the 2025 illustrative stack (AAA 62 / AA 11 / A 8 / BBB 5 / BB 5 / equity 9 percent, spreads 130/175/210/310/575 bp). Right: waterfall of the 319 bp WAS through 161 bp of note spreads, 45 bp fees and 75 bp expected loss to 38 bp for equity (4.2% over SOFR on a 9% slice), then a 50 bp repricing taking it to -12 bp (-1.3%, a 5.6-point fall). Data: Illustrative; the worked deal of s21.3.1 (WAS 319 bp from Loomis Sayles YE2025, AAA 130 bp; other spreads, fees and EL are the chapter's stated assumptions).
3. **Figure 21.3, ch21_ccc_cliff_and_cure.png** (s21.4.2, after the paragraph that ends "not yet a restructuring"). Left: CCC-excess haircut against CCC par as a share of non-defaulted par; flat to the 7.5% limit, then 1 - p* per dollar billed to the cheapest CCCs. Right: change in ACPA from selling each of the 21 CCC loans of the Listing 21.1 deal and reinvesting at par (richest +0.78mm, cheapest -0.03mm), with the lower bound of (21.6) dashed and the three loans inside the excess set in orange. Data: Illustrative; the functions are copied from Listing 21.1 (seed 21), the left-panel CCC ladder is stated in the caption.
4. **Figure 21.4, ch21_loan_short_call.png** (s21.5.1, after the "Negative convexity, measured" paragraph). Loan price against market spread minus margin: no-call line (21.2) and repricing-hazard lines h = 1 and h = 4 (21.7), kinked at par; slopes 2.7 / 0.9 / 0.25 points per 100 bp of tightening; the chapter's three worked prices (101.36, 100.45, 100.12) at m - s = 50 bp; the 101 soft-call cap. Data: Illustrative; (21.2) and (21.7) with T = 3 and a flat 6.65% discount rate, which reproduces the chapter's numbers exactly.
5. **Figure 21.5, ch21_buffer_queue.png** (s21.6.1, after the "scaling is the point" paragraph). 99% cash buffer against settlement lag for persistence 0, 0.4 and 0.7, with the 4.5% median bank-loan fund liquidity ratio and the T+1 / T+7 / observed T+20 lags; the phi = 0.4 curve gives the chapter's 1.07%, 4.16% and 7.77%. Data: Illustrative, (21.9) with 5 bp of NAV mean outflow and 0.40% innovation volatility at phi = 0.4 (same daily volatility for every phi); the 4.5% median is from the Fed FEDS Note (19 Aug 2026), as cited in the chapter.

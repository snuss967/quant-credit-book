## 1. The spine

The reader must feel one question by the end of the cold open: *if this bond is only 4.1% volatile, how did it lose fifty-two points in an afternoon, and why did a bond trading at 40 then climb back to 85?* The answer, built across the chapter, is that a high-yield return is two worlds and a coin: the wobble you measure while nobody is betting says nothing about the size of the pot. The chapter then keeps pulling on that thread. The coin is a menu (hard default, distressed exchange, re-default). The pot is a distribution, and since 2020 it depends on whether you signed. The sign of the skew flips when the bond trades near its recovery. And the tail tools everyone reaches for belong to the book, not the bond. Edition 1 states all of this; Edition 2 must let Theo discover it, mostly by being wrong first, against two real events that between them show the left jump (Serta, 2020–2026) and the right jump (Carvana, 2022–2023, with Hertz as the coda). The 13-week model's promotion to a hazard rate and the Fourteen-Sigma risk report (engagement.md §5 calls it the template and asks that it grow across Parts) are the recurring props.

## 1. The spine

By the end of the cold open the reader should feel one question in their stomach: **the dealer is not pricing 150 bonds, so what is it pricing, and who at the table already knows?** The chapter's answer unfolds in four terms. The lump can be hedged in a minute (fair value, hedge). The residual is where the PJT skills live (liquidation cost, residual covariance, eviction). The mystery box is where the PJT skills *win* (adverse selection by line, the hand history). And the twist that the RV thread adds: the client hiding the two cards may be a pod whose whole book *is* residual, so the dealer's engine and the pod's alpha model are pricing the same number from opposite sides of the screen. Three real episodes carry it: the H1 2022 reversal (eviction and margin), March 2020 against April 2025 (stress commonality and basis), and a live October 2026 basket whose hand history settles a week later.

Clocks (cast bible C1): the cold open is live, **Friday 9 Oct 2026, 2:58 p.m.**, Theo's week three; the lender call is Monday 12 Oct; the hand history settles Friday 16 Oct. The 2022 and the 2020/2025 scenes are **replays**, Ines's cut-the-tape ritual, labeled as such in the first italic line. Theo never trades in the pod; he sees the pod's basket as a market maker's counterparty (rv_thread rule 8).

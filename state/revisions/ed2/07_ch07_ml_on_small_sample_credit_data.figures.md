## Flags for the orchestrator (not fixed: outside the figure edit's remit)
1. Scale of the hat count. Section 7.6's worked example (800 parents, daily labels, N_eff about 63,000 from about 576,000 bond-months) and the cold open and Debrief (212,000 bond-months, N_eff about 24,000) are different panels but the same one-ninth ratio; Figure 7.3 uses the second, and its caption says so. It reconciles with three bonds per parent and monthly labels on a 13-week horizon (3 x 3 = 9), a derivation the chapter never states.
2. Theo's benchmark in the Debrief ("four-trial benchmark, SR* about 0.02", T = 96 months) does not follow from the Full Derivation's formula with V = 1/T, which gives about 0.11 a month for N = 4 and T = 96. It works only if V is the (small) cross-trial variance of four highly correlated sizing models, which the chapter does not say. Figure 7.5 uses V = 1/T, as the Full Derivation and Exercise 4 do, and does not draw Theo's case.

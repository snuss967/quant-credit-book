## A5 (RSSG red-pen 1-2): fixed

All changes in `book_src/90_appA_notation_and_conventions.md`, §A.9 (glossary).

- **Cram-down** (line 191): removed the contradicted joke "Nobody gets crammed up." Replaced with "Usually aimed down the stack at a junior class; aimed the other way, it is a cram-up." Definition clause otherwise unchanged.
- **Cram-up** (new entry, after Cram-down): confirmation over a senior secured class's objection, by reinstatement under Section 1124(2) (class unimpaired, no vote) or under Section 1129(b) via replacement notes at a rate the class calls too low. *Charter* (2009) = reinstatement case; *Momentive* (2d Cir. 2017) = rate case (efficient-market rate first, *Till* formula rate as fallback). Notes cheap legacy debt as an asset of the estate. Consistent with Ch 12-C (§1124 passage, which names Charter 2009 and cram-up).
- **Reinstatement** (new entry, alphabetical between Recovery lock and Reinvestment period): Section 1124(2) cure plus original maturity, class unimpaired and does not vote; value of the claim is the old coupon discounted at today's spread; cross-reference to cram-up.
- **Absolute priority rule** (line 171): added the qualifier that it binds in cram-down against a rejecting class, and that consensual deviations (tip to old equity, senior-class gift) are routine where every affected class votes yes; ties to the Franks-Torous 78 percent figure already in Chapter 6 (§6.2; the review cited §6.4, but the passage sits in §6.2) with "squares with", no new number; *DBSD* (2d Cir. 2011) cited for the limit once a class rejects (gift to old equity refused over an unsecured objector). Original hedge "Honored in principle, negotiated in practice" kept.

Note: *DBSD* is a gifting/absolute-priority case, not a cram-up case, so it is cited in the APR entry and not in Cram-up (the synthesis row grouped it with the cram-up cites).

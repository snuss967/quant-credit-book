## 1. The spine

The question the reader should feel at 00:11 on the countdown is the one the chapter already states in bold: **who is selling, why now, and what is that worth?** Edition 2 should make the reader feel a second, more personal version of it: *the thing I know how to do, read a credit agreement and know who was on the 2 p.m. call, is a number in somebody's bid, and nobody on this desk can compute it but me.* The chapter answers by showing that every bid is three bets (fair value, inventory, toxicity), that the shelf is one long argument about the third, and that the market is plumbing, not a crowd: forced sellers who know nothing (Ford, March 2020) are the desk's income, informed sellers who know everything (Serta, June 2020) are its ruin, and the ETF that was the only live price (LQD, 12 March 2020) is the case where the mid itself is the lie. The reader leaves able to say which of the three kinds of seller is on the other side of any RFQ, and what each one is worth.

## Embedded figures

1. **Figure 10.1, ch10_carry_hurdle.png** (s10.1.2, after the carry-hurdle paragraph, with one pointer sentence). Left: the hurdle (average spread / spread duration, 12-month window) against spread level for an HY-like (D = 4) and an IG-like (D = 7) book, with the chapter's worked points (112.5 bp, 14 bp). Right: the 12-month change in HY OAS against its own hurdle for the last two years, shading where a spread-change rule is short while a return rule is still long (150 vs 13 short days out of 534). Data: left Illustrative (formula of s10.1.2); right ICE BofA US HY OAS via FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2 (derived series cached), approximate 12-month return = mean OAS - 4 x change in OAS.
2. **Figure 10.2, ch10_dts_vol_scaling.png** (s10.3.1, after the "P&L splits into a sign part and a timing part" paragraph, with one pointer sentence). Left: volatility of daily OAS changes against average OAS for seven ICE BofA rating buckets (AAA to CCC) in 12 calendar quarters (84 points, log-log), with the through-the-origin line vol = 0.23 x OAS (log-log slope 0.86, R^2 0.62) and the Q2 2025 points circled; IG buckets sit above the line. Right: notional that holds risk constant through April 2025 under a DTS forecast (beta x D x s) and a 60-day EWMA forecast, 2 April 2025 = 1.0 (DTS notional 0.74 on 7 April, the 342 -> 461 bp arithmetic of Ines's question at the start of s10.3). Data: ICE BofA OAS via FRED (BAMLC0A1CAAA, BAMLC0A2CAA, BAMLC0A3CA, BAMLC0A4CBBB, BAMLH0A1HYBB, BAMLH0A2HYB, BAMLH0A3HYC, BAMLH0A0HYM2), derived statistics cached; D = 4 assumed; beta (0.305 for HY OAS) estimated on 2023-24 quarters only.
   NOTE FOR THE CHAPTER'S REVISER (text vs data, no chapter text was changed): the Ways-to-Blow-Up box in s10.3.2 says that in April 2025 "a 60-day EWMA had about three new observations" while "a DTS forecast had the new spread on day one", implying that the EWMA lags. On the real HY OAS the opposite happens on the gap days: the three squared returns of 3-7 April are so large that an EWMA with centre of mass 60 days cuts the constant-risk notional to 0.61 by 4 April (DTS: 0.77), and then keeps it cut for two months (0.70 on 30 June against 1.16 for DTS). Figure 10.2 (right) shows this honestly. The box's conclusion (use the max of EWMA, DTS and a floor; DTS re-risks sooner) still holds, but the "EWMA lags in a gap" sentence is not supported by the 2025 data.
3. **Figure 10.3, ch10_trend_smile.png** (s10.6.1, after the Fung-Hsieh / Hamill paragraph that follows the small-move parabola, with one pointer sentence). Left: closed-form E[r_TF | r] = r[2 Phi(rho r / (sigma sqrt(1 - rho^2))) - 1] for rho = 0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5 against the |r| straddle limit, with the small-move parabola for rho = 0.25. Right: 400 simulated rounds at rho = 0.25 (each pays +|r| or -|r|), the closed-form curve and eight bin means with one-standard-error bars. Data: Illustrative (bivariate normal of s10.6.1); the script asserts the closed form against a 2-million-draw simulation. A regression of quarterly trend P&L on the Federal Reserve Board's GZ spread (approximate returns, 1973-2026) was tried and rejected as a figure: the quadratic term flips sign with the lookback (t = +5.2, -6.6, +1.2 for 3, 6, 12 months), so no honest "empirical smile" could be shown.
4. **Figure 10.4, ch10_whipsaw_capture.png** (s10.6.2, after the whipsaw-arithmetic paragraph, with one pointer sentence). Left: the stylized V (T = 20 months, preceded by a slow tightening) with the positions of a 3-month (L = 1.5) and a 12-month (L = 6) signal as long/short ribbons and their captures (+70%, -20%, the paragraph's numbers). Right: share of the round trip captured against the V's duration T on a log axis for lags 0.5, 1.5 and 6 months, with the March 2020 / April 2025 band (T about 1.5-2) and the 2007-09 line (T = 24); break-even dots at T = 4L. Data: Illustrative (the stylized V of s10.6.2). The script checks the closed form against a brute-force integration of the position on both sides of T = 2L. Extension beyond the chapter's text: the chapter's (T - 4L)/T holds for T >= 2L; for L < T < 2L (signal flips after the trough) the same four legs give 2L/T - 2, and for T <= L the signal never flips inside the V (capture 0). The figure plots all three pieces.
5. **Figure 10.5, ch10_episodes_baa_spread.png** (s10.7, after the intro paragraph that precedes Case Study A, with one pointer sentence). Small multiples of the four case-study episodes (A 2007-09, B Feb-Jun 2020, C 2022, D Apr 2025), each a 30-month window on a shared y axis and one time scale, with the episode window shaded, the peak labelled (6.16% on 4 Dec 2008, 4.31% on 23 Mar 2020, 2.42% on 1 Jul 2022, 2.02% on 7 Apr 2025), the SG Trend result the chapter quotes for the episode, and scale bars for a 3-month and a 12-month lookback. Data: FRED BAA10Y (Moody's Baa yield less 10-year Treasury), daily, cached from 2007 (tools/figures/data/ch10_BAA10Y.csv); SG Trend returns exactly as in s10.7 (+20.9% 2008, +6.24% 2020, +27.4% 2022 per the chapter's Cambridge Associates cite, -4.9% April 2025; the trend dossier's Hedgeweek figure for 2022 is +27.3%). The chapter's HY OAS cannot be used for 2008, 2020 or 2022 because FRED now serves only the last three years of ICE BofA series.

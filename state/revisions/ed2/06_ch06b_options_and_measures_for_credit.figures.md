- **Figure 6-B.2, ch06b_gamma_rent.png** (6-B.2.4, after "This is the most useful equation in options trading"): left, daily gamma rent against spot for a 1-week, 3-month and 1-year option when realized vol is 40% and implied 30% (peak 9.2 cents a day for one week against 1.2 cents for one year); right, mean and 5th-95th percentile of the daily-hedged P&L of a 3-month at-the-money call against realized vol (mean crosses zero at the 30% implied; about 2 per option for every 10 vol points). Data: Illustrative, Black-Scholes at 30% implied, 4% rates, strike 100; 20,000 lognormal paths, 63 daily hedges, seed 66, common random numbers across the realized-vol grid.
- **Figure 6-B.3, ch06b_merton_bond_greeks.png** (6-B.3.4, after "Three readings"): bond delta and bond gamma against asset value V (40 to 170) for the 216 bp firm (F = 80, vol 25%) and the CCC cousin (F = 95, vol 40%), marked at V = 100 (delta 0.12 and 0.33; gamma -0.0080 and -0.0091, matching the table). Gamma is negative everywhere and deepest below the face value (V near 70). Data: Illustrative, merton() of Listing 6-B.1.
- **Figure 6-B.4, ch06b_jtd_put_floor_and_wing.png** (6-B.5.5, after "The default probability alone puts a floor under the wing"): left, put price per dollar of strike against K/F0 on a log axis with and without the jump, flattening at the unit recovery claim 0.0469; right, Black-Scholes implied vol of the jump puts (about 101% at K/F0 = 0.2) against the wing bound (6B.3) (78.5% at K/F0 = 0.2, the chapter's number) and the flat 40% diffusion line. The script asserts the bound at every strike drawn. Data: Illustrative, Merton (1976) jump to zero, S0 = 100, vol 40%, r = 4%, lambda = 5%, T = 1; jtd_price(), implied_vol(), wing_floor_vol() of Listing 6-B.1.
- **Figure 6-B.5, ch06b_halvorsen_two_measures.png** (6-B.8 step 4, after "The disagreement is not about whether Halvorsen defaults"): left, survival under P (hazard 6.19%) and Q (14.5%), one-year points 94.0% and 86.5%, xi = 2.34; right, waterfall of the 1,150 bp spread into 371 expected loss, 499 hazard premium and 280 recovery premium. Data: Illustrative (fictional Halvorsen), the chapter's inputs through MeasuredPD.hazard() and spread_decomposition() of Listing 6-B.1.

## 1. The spine

The cold open already asks Ines's question ("What's the pot?") and Theo already gets it half right: the pot is not \$2bn. The reader should finish the cold open feeling a sharper version: **on the week when every 5% line on the Street is hit at once, will my alpha own this book or will the rule?** Every section is one clause of the answer. Risk, not dollars, is the pot (§28.1). The pot is smaller than it looks because issuers cluster (§28.2) and because every signal pays rake in its tier (§28.3). A single default can end the game, so size with a ruin boundary the documents set (§28.4). The bid moves away from the mid before anything happens to the issuer, so the mark is a stop you did not write (§28.5–28.6). Charge both inside the leverage band and the band may be empty, which is the honest answer (§28.7). The people who widen the band are the ones whose information the price factors do not contain (§28.8), and the platform pays for exactly that residual and nothing else (new Dov Adler review). The 13-week model, reborn as the dashboard, is the credit agreement Theo writes for his own desk (§28.9). Cast bible C2 is canon: this is "Desk Head for a Day", Ines lending the lanyard as a drill, and the cold open must say so in one line.

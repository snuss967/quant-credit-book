All five are drawn by tools/figures/ch04c.py (run: python3 tools/figures/ch04c.py). Seats keep one colour in every figure (dealer blue, systematic orange, pod aqua, CLO yellow, insurer magenta, direct lender green, opportunistic fund violet; the desk is ink). - **Figure 4-C.2, ch04c_horizon_flips_sign.png** (4C.1, after the Math, Gently box on horizon; NEW, with one pointer sentence): left, expected gain 0.036T against typical swing 1.5 sqrt(T) over a year, marking Dana's 20 days and the pod's 252; right, expected drift collected, exit cost and net for Dana (5-point stop, 20 days: +0.5, -1.3, -0.7) and the pod (15-point stop, one year: +6.7, -1.2, +5.5). Data: Illustrative, the box's random-walk model (drift 9 points a year, daily noise 1.5, 3 points per exit), stop-touch probability by the reflection principle with drift (0.42 and 0.41), expected time in the trade as the integral of survival.

## 3. Fixes made in the chapter

1. **Wrong speaker (hard-rule fix).** §17B.1 View From Another Seat gave "high 30% to low 40%" to "Ares Capital's president Jim Miller". The MarketBeat transcript and the perspectives dossier put it in CEO Kort Schnabel's prepared remarks, about 2025 first-lien originations ("maintaining LTVs in the high 30% to low 40% range"). Now attributed to Schnabel with the verified fragment.
2. **Wrong quotation (Apollo).** The Trend Watch quoted "any investment, subscription, redemption, or transaction decision" as the release's words. The release says the number "is not intended to constitute a definitive valuation or to serve as a basis for any investment decision" and not for subscriptions, redemptions or transactions. Rewritten.
3. **Wrong date and arithmetic (position size).** Blue Owl's Pluralsight position was 1.7% of OBDC's net assets at 31 Dec 2023 (KBRA's $100.9mm fair value against ~$6bn of net assets), not in March 2024; at 83.5 it was ~1.5%. The NAV effect is now 1.7% x (96.8 - 47.0)/96.8 = 0.87% (was 0.74%), in §17B.5, the debrief table and exercise 5 (check "about 0.87%; about a fourteenth"). The conclusion (smaller than a day's move) stands.
4. **Anachronism and "seven holders" (KBRA).** KBRA published on 5 June 2024, after the 25 Apr 2024 committee, and its appendix lists 15 BDC vehicles from seven managers (five Blue Owl vehicles, two Goldman, two BlackRock, two Oaktree, two Golub, plus Franklin BSP and Ares). §17B.1 now says "the BDCs of the seven managers", dated as a tally KBRA published that June; Figure 17-B.1 caption and Resource Graph entry updated (KBRA dated 5 June 2024).
5. **Goodwin quotation.** The words are "Vista's drop-down was one of, if not, the first sponsor funded LME"; "in private credit" is not in the visible sentence. Text now attributes the quote to the drop-down and drops the unsourced tail. "Pre-signing alert" replaced by "alert of 12 August".

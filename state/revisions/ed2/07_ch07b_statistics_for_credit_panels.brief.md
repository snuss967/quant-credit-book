## 1. The spine

The question the reader should feel by the end of the cold open is Ines's: **"How many times have you really seen this card?"** Every number that comes off a credit panel (a t-statistic, a PD, a half-life, a covariance, a simulated tail) is a count of evidence, and credit panels count the same evidence many times: eleven CUSIPs of one issuer, twelve overlapping months of one return, four hundred bonds sharing one March, a cohort of defaults that is one draw of the common factor. The chapter answers it tool by tool, and each tool is the same move wearing a different tie: find the unit that is independent, count only those, and attach the error bar you would defend to Ines. The cold open poses it on Theo's runway signal; Scene 1 (§7-B.1–7-B.2) poses it on the spring of 2020, when the whole panel shared one month; Scene 2 (§7-B.4) poses it on the real LMT re-default cohort, where the clock stops before the story ends; §7-B.5–7-B.8 pose it on covariance, resampling and simulation; §7-B.9 grades Theo's card. The Debrief's Finder table is the reference layer (lesson 11).

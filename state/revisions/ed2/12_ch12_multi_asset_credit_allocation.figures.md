Figures 12.4 and 12.5 plot these series and the script caches the plotted slice (daily OAS over the rolling three-year window; total-return values only for the dates used) in tools/figures/data/ch12_ice_fred.csv. Decide before publication whether to keep the figures with the FRED/ICE citation, ask ICE for permission, or drop the cache and the two figures (the chapter text already quotes the individual levels). The license-free alternative is the Gilchrist-Zakrajsek series in the same dossier, which has no CCC/BB split.

No discrepancy found.

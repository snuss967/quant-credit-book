# Figures log: Chapter 12-C (ed2 U4)

Plan (no ed2 brief file existed; chose from the chapter text and state/advisor_briefs/12_ch12c_*.md section "Figures"): the chapter already embedded three figures (12C.1 to 12C.3, numbered without a hyphen) whose PNGs were not on disk, so those three were drawn to the captions' stated numbers, and two new figures were added for the DIP roll-up (12C.4) and the recovery distributions (12C.10). No dossier data series apply: Halvorsen is fictional, so every figure is Illustrative, drawn from the chapter's own model (plan value ~ 0.7 LN(1,300; 0.10) + 0.3 LN(1,900; 0.12); the 12B.9 / 12C.10 silo waterfall). No external data were fetched.

All five are drawn by tools/figures/ch12c.py, which first re-derives the chapter's printed numbers and stops on a mismatch (fulcrum 82.8% / 17.2%; class recoveries 59.2 / 3.4 at V = 1,300; conditional and mixture expected recoveries 59.7 / 95.2 / 70.3 and 3.5 / 19.4 / 8.3; roll-up 4.2 and 9.5 points; take-back 13.3 per 100bp and 26.5 at 200bp). Each PNG was viewed and cleaned (label collisions, a leader line crossing text, a note colliding with a mean line, escaped dollar signs).

## Embedded figures

- **Figure 12-C.1, ch12c_call_spreads.png** (§12C.2, after the "Kaz's question" paragraph; was already embedded as 12C.1, PNG was missing): top panel, dollars paid to each class against plan value V as stacked bands (ABL + DIP + admin 525, first lien 1,250, unsecured pool 1,020, old equity); each band's thickness is a call spread, with V* = 1,858 and 2,795 marked. Bottom panel, the mixture density on the same x axis with the fulcrum probabilities shaded: 82.8% first lien (blue), 17.2% unsecured (orange). The density sits in a shared-x panel under the bands rather than on a twin axis (house rule: no twinx), so the caption's "overlaid" is read as "aligned". Data: Illustrative, §12C.1 mixture and §12C.10 waterfall.
- **Figure 12-C.2, ch12c_rollup_transfer.png** (§12C.4, new; after the "Count dollars instead of points" paragraph, with one pointer sentence appended to it): left, recovery of the rolled paper (65.0 to 100.0) and of the 1,250 left behind (65.0 to 60.8), each with a gain / loss of $52.5mm; right, left-behind recovery against the amount rolled up (kappa M), no-roll-up baseline 65.0, markers at 1:1 (60.8, -4.2 points) and 2:1 (55.5, -9.5 points), and the W = 910 guard where the closed form is refused. Data: Illustrative, R - R' = kM(F - W) / (F(F - kM)) with F = 1,400, W = 910.
- **Figure 12-C.3, ch12c_recovery_distributions.png** (§12C.10, new; after the "Look at the shape before the numbers" paragraph, with a pointer sentence appended): first-lien recovery in 2.5-point bins with the 17.2% point mass at par and mean 70.3; unsecured-pool recovery in 37 log-spaced bins (hump near 3, tail to 100, mean 8.3); each bar split by which expert the judge adopts (0.7 debtor, 0.3 committee). Computed analytically from the mixture CDF, not simulated. Data: Illustrative, §12C.10.
- **Figure 12-C.4, ch12c_fulcrum_prob_vs_dip.png** (§12C.10, after "The DIP moves the fulcrum; the roll-up does not"; was already embedded as 12C.2, PNG was missing): fulcrum probability of the first lien and the unsecured pool against DIP new money M from 0 to 400, with the points the caption cites (75/25 at M = 0, 83/17 at 150, 90/10 at 280); V*(M) = 250 + (1,320 + M)(35/32), top strike 2,645 + M. Data: Illustrative, §12C.10.
- **Figure 12-C.5, ch12c_takeback_transfer.png** (§12C.10, after "The take-back, priced"; was already embedded as 12C.3, PNG was missing): transfer to the equity electors against the coupon gap, first-order line F x 3.79 x gap ($13.3mm per 100bp) and the exact annuity price F x gap x a5(8% + gap), marked at the plan's 200bp gap, $26.5mm. Reading chosen so the caption holds: the plan coupon is fixed at 8.0% and the court or market yield is 8.0% + gap, so the exact curve bends below the first-order line (about $1mm at 300bp, $2.6mm at 400bp) and the two cross at 200bp. If the market yield were held at 10% and the coupon varied, the exact price is exactly linear and the two lines would coincide. Data: Illustrative, §12C.5 and §12B.6.

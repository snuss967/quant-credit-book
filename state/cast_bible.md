## Rules for writers

1. Before giving Mags, Ines, Kaz or Theo a war story, backstory or position, search this file; add the new fact (chapter, event, position, outcome) in the same commit.
2. One telling per fact. Later chapters point back ("Mags's notebook, Ch 14 §14.8.1") instead of retelling.
3. Live scenes name the story week; anything before 22 Sep 2026 is a labeled replay.
4. Never attach Theo to a named real PJT mandate, give Ines a named poker result, or name Mags's employers.
5. Never give a fictional character conduct a real participant could be thought to have done. No cast member sits in a real ad hoc group, majority or co-op, signs a real co-op, trades while restricted, or leaks. Cast positions in real deals are outside-holder or market-maker positions, labeled (illustrative), with the fiction label once per scene. All dialogue belongs to the cast; real people get verified facts only.
6. Gags: one Kaz misread per term; the co-op counter moves only on a signing shown on the page; ROBO stays literal.
7. Pronouns: Theo he, Ines she, Mags she, Kaz they, ROBO it. New fictional issuers get a roster line here; never alter an existing instrument's terms.

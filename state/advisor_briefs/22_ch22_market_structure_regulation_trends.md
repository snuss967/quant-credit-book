**Treasury clearing mandate (VERIFIED, SEC press release 2023-247, fetched 2026-10-17).** Adopted 2023-12-13. Covered clearing agencies must require members to clear (i) all Treasury repo/reverse repo a member enters into (exceptions: state/local governments, other clearing organizations, inter-affiliate), and (ii) cash Treasury purchases/sales where a member is an interdealer broker, or between a clearing member and a registered broker-dealer / government securities broker or dealer. Exempt counterparties: central banks, sovereigns, international financial institutions, natural persons. Original dates: phase one by 2025-03-31 (house/customer margin separation, customer-protection rule changes, access for indirect participants); cash by 2025-12-31; repo by 2026-06-30. Gensler: Treasury market "$26 trillion"; "70 to 80 percent of the Treasury funding market is uncleared, and at least 80 percent of the cash market is uncleared."

**Treasury clearing extension (VERIFIED, SEC press release 2025-43, 2025-02-25).** Cash compliance extended to **2026-12-31**; repo to **2027-06-30**; margin-separation (Rule 17ad-22(e)(6)(i)) enforcement moved to 2025-09-30. Acting Chairman Uyeda: "This one-year extension provides additional time to implement and validate operational changes." SEARCH-LISTED (2026-10-17): FIA paper (Apr 2026) repeats 2026-12-31 cash date; CME Securities Clearing approved by SEC ~2025-12-01/02 (sources differ by a day, CHECK); ICE approval 2026-01-30 (FIA paper, CHECK); FICC remains incumbent; Treasury TBAC Q1-2026 charge lists "done-away market structure" among open issues. No further delay found as of 2026-10-17 (CHECK before print).

**T+1 (VERIFIED, SEC press release 2023-29).** Adopted 2023-02-15; compliance 2024-05-28; T+2 → T+1 for most broker-dealer transactions; same-day affirmation "as soon as technologically practicable and no later than the end of trade date"; RIA recordkeeping of allocations/confirmations/affirmations; CMSP (central matching service provider) STP policies plus annual report. Gensler: "it will reduce latency, lower risk, and promote efficiency."

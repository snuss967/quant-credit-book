# Advisor Brief: `00_02_how_to_use_this_book.md`

*How to Use This Book: The Ramp, the Gates, the Ninety Days Before Day One, and How to Study With the Robot.* Front matter, 3,000–5,000 words, placed after the preface. Not a chapter: no anatomy check, no Specification box, no Resource Graph. Warm and direct; the reader is the person who has an offer (or wants one) and a book that is 500k words long. Sources below are the book's own dossiers and files; no web research was done. Fable advisor, 18 Oct 2026.

## 1. Thesis and the "aha"

The preface already tells the reader *what* is in the book and now (D6 landed) gives four seat paths and Theo's origin paragraph (`book_src/00_00_preface.md` lines 32–50, 56–58). What no file does is tell the reader where they are, what they must be able to *do* before moving on, how to check themselves, and how to study with the same AI agent the book teaches them to direct. `00_02` is that file. Its thesis, in one sentence: **the book is a course disguised as a reference, and the exercises with their asserts are the grader; your job is to arrive at the gate with the number, not the feeling.**

The "aha" is the one the transition coach named (`state/reviews/ed2/review_transition_coach.md` §1, problem 1): "A desk would call it trading without a markout." The reader's study method should be the desk's method. Every chapter already has a markout (the asserts in its code; the Ch 5-style parenthetical "Check:" numbers); the ramp, the tiers and the gates are what let a career-changer studying alone tell whether they are ready for Chapter 14 before they open it. The second aha, from §26.9 item 8 (`book_src/26_ch26_ai_agents_and_llms_on_the_credit_desk.md` line 528, already rewritten with the learning corollary, D9): the METR 2025 result means a reader who lets ROBO do the exercises will *feel* fast and learn less, so the Spec box is the prompt and the asserts are the grade.

Do not duplicate the preface's seat paths; deepen them. The preface gives the route; `00_02` gives the route with tiers, hours, the primers' section numbers, the gates at each Part boundary, and what to skip on a given tier.

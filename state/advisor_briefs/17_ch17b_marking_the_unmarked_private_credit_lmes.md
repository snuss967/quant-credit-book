## 1. Thesis and the aha

**Thesis.** A private mark is a moving average of a truth that other people's filings reveal first. Because every large private loan sits in several public vehicles, and every semi-liquid vehicle must publish its tender results, the asset class that has no tape leaves a paper trail dense enough to build a default detector on, and the detector's inputs are the same beats an RSSG banker already knows: the PIK amendment, the sponsor's equity mark, the collateral move, the non-accrual footnote, the re-cut. The queue is the same object from the investor's side: a censored, autocorrelated belief series the manager does not control.

**The aha** (hand it to Theo in Scene 4): *the 10-Q is a docket.* The restructuring he used to run from a data room is readable, late and in fragments, from the schedules of investments of seven BDCs, and the fragments disagree with each other in a way that is itself the signal. Cross-holder dispersion is not noise around a true mark; it is the distribution of lenders' private information, published quarterly. And the second aha, for the pod: *the only short is a proxy, and the proxy shorts the wrong thing.* Marcus's question ("what is the book long that it did not mean to be?") has an answer here that no hedge ratio fixes: the book is long the mark's lag.
